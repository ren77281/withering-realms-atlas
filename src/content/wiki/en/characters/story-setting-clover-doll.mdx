The **Withering Realms story** begins with mysteries that promotional material intentionally leaves unanswered. The confirmed foundation is clear: the year is 1926, a world-changing occult event occurred ten years earlier, and nine-year-old Clover awakens alone in a decaying mansion near its epicenter. She discovers she is a ghost. Her companion is a powerful doll guardian made from wood, metal, and human remains, with a missing right arm designed for crafted weapons and machines. Together they explore Penwyll while looking for a way to bring Clover back to life. This guide organizes that premise without turning suggestive wording into invented lore or spoilers.

## What is the story premise of Withering Realms?

The game is set in **1926**, ten years after an immense occult event halted the Great War and transformed the world overnight. The dead now walk, spirits roam, and some gifted people can cast spells. Clover's starting mansion lies only miles from the event's epicenter, tying her personal mystery to the larger setting.

## Who is Clover?

Clover is nine years old. She awakens alone, realizes she is a ghost, and travels through Penwyll and its surroundings while seeking a way to return to life. The official sources do not explain how she died, why she awakened, or what connection she had to the mansion before the story begins.

## What is the doll guardian?

Clover's only companion at the start is a strange and powerful doll constructed from **wood, metal, and human remains**. Its missing right arm accepts crafted weapons and machines, linking a story detail to a confirmed equipment system.

Its creator, origin, consciousness, and exact relationship to Clover have not been publicly explained. Those questions are part of the story's uncertainty and should not be filled with fan theories in a factual guide.

## Where does the story take place?

Penwyll is the central mysterious town, with shops and NPCs. Graveyards sprawl around it, while official descriptions also name tombs, caves, and rotting estates as exploration spaces. ## What remains intentionally unexplained?

Pre-release sources do not reveal the occult event's cause, Clover's death, the doll's maker, or the solution to Penwyll's mystery. They also do not explain why Clover is connected to the epicenter or how returning to life might work. These are story questions, not missing database fields.
