## What is the story premise of Withering Realms?

The game opens in **1926**, ten years after an immense occult event stopped the Great War and transformed the world overnight. The dead now walk, spirits roam, and some gifted people can cast spells. The central mystery begins close to the event's epicenter.

## Who is Clover?

Clover is nine years old. She awakens alone in a decaying mansion, then discovers that she is a ghost. Her journey through Penwyll and its surrounding graveyards is driven by a search for a way to bring her back to life.

## What is the doll guardian?

Clover's only companion at the start is described as a strange and powerful doll constructed from **wood, metal, and human remains**. The doll has a missing right arm that can accept crafted weapons and machines. Its creator, origin, and exact relationship to Clover have not been publicly explained.

See the [doll arm equipment guide](/items/doll-arm-weapons) for the attachments currently named by official sources.

## How does this story connect to Withering Rooms?

The official FAQ calls it a new, independent story set in the same universe as Withering Rooms. Players are not expected to know the previous game to understand or enjoy Withering Realms. The dedicated [Withering Realms sequel guide](/guides/is-withering-realms-a-sequel) separates that confirmed relationship from crossover details that remain unannounced.

## What remains intentionally unexplained?

Pre-release sources do not reveal the occult event's cause, Clover's death, the doll's maker, or the solution to Penwyll's mystery. Those are story questions, not missing guide data. This page avoids turning trailer imagery or marketing language into plot spoilers.
