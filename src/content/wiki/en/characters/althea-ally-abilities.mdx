## Who is Althea in Withering Realms?

Althea is a **daring freelance investigator** pursuing Penwyll's dark secret. Official feature text presents her as an example of the allies Clover and her doll guardian can make during the game. It does not yet publish her biography, quest chain, or relationship to other characters.

## Which abilities are associated with Althea?

Moonless Formless names three methods Althea is willing to use: interrogation, lockpicking, and forgery.

## Is Althea playable or a combat companion?

No official source currently confirms either claim. “Make allies” can describe quest support, services, story cooperation, or active companionship. Until gameplay documentation identifies her role, this page will not label Althea playable or assign her combat abilities.

## How does Althea connect to Penwyll?

Her stated goal is to uncover the town's dark secret. Penwyll is the central town with shops and NPCs, while the wider region includes graveyards, tombs, caves, and rotting estates. The [Penwyll locations guide](/maps/penwyll-confirmed-locations) records that confirmed geography.
