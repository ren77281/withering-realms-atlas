Official **Withering Realms controller support** is broader than a generic “controller recommended” label: Steam's public app data lists Full controller support and includes separate DualShock and DualSense categories. That is useful for players planning to use a PlayStation controller on PC, but it does not answer every input question. The store has not documented exact button prompts, remapping options, wireless behavior, or support for every Xbox and third-party model. Steam also does not currently show a Deck compatibility rating, so the game should not be called Verified or Playable before Valve publishes one. This guide separates those official store fields from the practical details that can only be checked after the Early Access build becomes available.

## Does Withering Realms have full controller support?

Yes. Steam currently sets the game's controller-support field to **Full** and lists Full controller support among its feature categories. It also lists DualShock and DualSense support.

“Full controller support” is Steam's product feature label. It should not be expanded into claims about haptics, adaptive triggers, glyph switching, dead-zone sliders, or a particular controller model unless those details appear in official documentation or repeatable testing.

## Is Withering Realms Steam Deck Verified?

No Steam Deck compatibility rating is currently shown for Withering Realms. That means **Verified**, **Playable**, and **Unsupported** are all inappropriate labels until Valve assigns a result. Windows support and full controller support are positive inputs, but neither proves Proton compatibility, readable text, stable performance, or complete access to every menu.

The published [Withering Realms system requirements](/guides/system-requirements-pc-specs) are desktop Windows specifications. They do not contain a Steam Deck frame-rate target or recommended settings.

## Will Xbox controllers work?

The Full controller support field indicates that the game is designed to be usable with a controller, but Steam's current categories explicitly name only DualShock and DualSense families. The store does not yet document Xbox glyphs, model-specific behavior, or remapping.

This does not mean Xbox controllers are unsupported; it means the official evidence is incomplete. A responsible compatibility answer requires either a developer statement or launch testing with a named controller and connection method.

## What should Steam Deck players check at launch?

Use a short checklist rather than relying on pre-release guesses:

1. Open the official Steam page and look for Valve's Deck compatibility badge.
2. Confirm that the game launches and reaches normal gameplay through the current Proton version.
3. Check menu navigation, button prompts, text size, and any required keyboard input.
4. Test frame pacing, battery use, suspend and resume, and several demanding areas.
5. Record the game version, Proton version, settings, and resolution with any performance result.

## Withering Realms controller FAQ

### Does Withering Realms support DualSense?

Yes. DualSense Controller Support is listed in Steam's current app categories.

### Does it support DualShock controllers?

Yes. Steam separately lists DualShock Controller Support.

### Are adaptive triggers or haptics confirmed?

No. The controller categories do not specify DualSense feature use.

### Can every action be remapped?

Full remapping has not been documented. Check the released options menu before assuming it is available.

### Can I play only with a controller?

Steam's Full controller support label is the strongest current indication, but launch testing is still needed to confirm edge cases such as text entry or reporting tools.

## What is confirmed before launch?

Withering Realms has official Full controller support plus DualShock and DualSense listings. Steam Deck status, exact prompts, advanced controller features, remapping, and model-by-model compatibility remain open. For the only confirmed launch platform, read the [Withering Realms PC and console status guide](/guides/platforms-console-release).
