## How can you avoid unofficial downloads and fake keys?

Start from `witheringrealms.com` or the Steam listing and follow links outward. Before installing or redeeming anything, confirm the Steam app ID is **3441990** and the developer and publisher are both Moonless Formless.

Avoid pages that offer an executable, repacked demo, paid beta key, account login, or survey in exchange for access. A file can use the correct game name and artwork without being authorized. The absence of a current official demo is a reason to wait, not to find an alternative build.
