## When does Withering Realms enter Early Access?

Withering Realms is scheduled to launch in Steam Early Access on **September 2, 2026**. The date appears on both the official game website and the Steam store listing. This is an Early Access release for Windows PC, not the final multi-platform launch.

| Release detail | Official status |
| --- | --- |
| Early Access date | September 2, 2026 |
| Early Access platform | Windows PC via Steam |
| Developer and publisher | Moonless Formless |
| Full-release date | Not announced |
| Named console versions | Not announced |

## What will the Early Access version include?

Moonless Formless says the Early Access build will be **playable from start to finish**. The developer also plans to add side quests and end-game modes before full release, while changing game systems and balance in response to Early Access feedback.

That wording is more specific than a typical unfinished-campaign launch, but it does not mean every optional activity or balance value is final. Our [gameplay overview](/guides/gameplay-overview) separates the confirmed launch structure from features that still lack public details.

## When will the full version launch?

No full-release date or Early Access duration has been announced. The official website confirms that console releases are planned after Early Access concludes, but it does not identify the consoles or provide a release window. See the [console and PS5 status page](/guides/platforms-console-release) for the exact wording.

## What may change during Early Access?

The developer explicitly lists two areas that may evolve: **game systems** and **balance**. Specific weapons, item effects, enemy values, routes, and progression advice should therefore be treated as version-sensitive once players gain access. We will date every gameplay update and avoid presenting pre-release trailer observations as tested mechanics.
