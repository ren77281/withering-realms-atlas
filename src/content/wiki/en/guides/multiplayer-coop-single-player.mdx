Players asking **“Is Withering Realms multiplayer?”** often see Clover and her doll guardian together and wonder whether the two characters form a co-op pair. The official listings do not support that interpretation. Steam currently classifies Withering Realms as Single-player and does not list online multiplayer, online co-op, local co-op, shared-screen play, or PvP. The doll is central to combat and progression, but the developer describes it as Clover's guardian and as the platform for crafted arm weapons, not as a second player slot. Early Access feedback may change systems and balance, yet no multiplayer feature has been announced. This page records the current player-mode evidence and explains what would need to change before co-op could be treated as confirmed.

## Is Withering Realms multiplayer?

No multiplayer mode is currently announced. Steam lists **Single-player** as the only player-mode category for Withering Realms.

“Not listed” is more precise than “technically impossible” or “cancelled.” It means the official store and website do not currently advertise the mode. If that changes, Steam's feature categories and a developer announcement would provide the clearest evidence.

## Can two players control Clover and the doll guardian?

Official descriptions present Clover as a ghost and the doll as her powerful guardian. Players craft weapons and machines for the doll's missing right arm, including devices such as a machinegun or flamethrower. Nothing in those descriptions assigns each character to a separate player.

The pair is therefore part of the story and gameplay structure, not evidence of co-op. Our [Withering Realms gameplay overview](/guides/gameplay-overview) covers the confirmed exploration, crafting, world-expansion, and night-revisit systems without inventing control roles.

## What does the single-player structure include?

The official material describes a solo action RPG journey through Penwyll and its surroundings. Its confirmed structure includes exploration, combat, persistent equipment and story progress, selected randomized areas, and crafted doll-arm equipment.

Steam also lists Achievements, Steam Cloud, Full controller support, and Family Sharing. These are platform features rather than multiplayer modes. Steam Cloud, for example, can synchronize a solo save without allowing another player to join.

## Could co-op be added during Early Access?

Moonless Formless says community feedback will help shape balance and identify areas needing improvement. The Steam Early Access description also plans additional end-game features and modes. Neither statement announces co-op, multiplayer, or PvP.

It is reasonable to watch for changes, but not to convert a broad “modes” statement into a specific network feature. Multiplayer would affect networking, saves, balance, interface, and store categories, so reliable confirmation should be explicit.

## Withering Realms multiplayer FAQ

### Does Withering Realms have online co-op?

No online co-op category or developer announcement currently exists.

### Does it support local co-op or split screen?

Local co-op and shared-screen play are not listed on Steam.

### Is there PvP?

No PvP mode is announced or listed.

### Is the doll controlled by a second player?

Official descriptions do not assign the doll to a second player. It is Clover's guardian and carries crafted arm equipment.

### Does Steam Cloud mean the game is online multiplayer?

No. Steam Cloud synchronizes supported data such as saves; it does not determine the player count.

## What is the current answer?

Withering Realms is a single-player game based on every current official player-mode listing. Online co-op, local co-op, multiplayer, and PvP are unannounced.
