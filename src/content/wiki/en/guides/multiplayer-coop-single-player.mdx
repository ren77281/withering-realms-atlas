## Is Withering Realms a multiplayer game?

No multiplayer mode is currently announced. Steam lists **Single-player** as the game's player-mode feature and does not list multiplayer or PvP.

| Mode | Current official status |
| --- | --- |
| Single-player | Listed on Steam |
| Online co-op | Not listed |
| Local or shared-screen co-op | Not listed |
| Online multiplayer | Not listed |
| PvP | Not listed |

## Can two players control Clover and the doll guardian?

Official descriptions consistently present Clover and her doll guardian as the central pair, but they do not describe two-player control. The doll's missing right arm accepts crafted weapons and machines, making it part of the game's equipment and combat systems rather than evidence of a second player slot.

For the confirmed gameplay loop, see the [Withering Realms gameplay overview](/guides/gameplay-overview) and [doll arm weapons guide](/items/doll-arm-weapons).

## Could co-op be added during Early Access?

Moonless Formless says game systems and balance will change with feedback during Early Access, but that statement does **not** announce co-op. A multiplayer feature should only be treated as confirmed if it appears on the official website, Steam feature list, or a linked developer account.

## Does single-player support Steam Cloud?

Yes. Steam currently lists Steam Cloud alongside Single-player, Steam Achievements, Full controller support, and Family Sharing. Those platform features do not change the game's single-player classification.

Official sources checked August 26, 2026: [Withering Realms on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3441990/Withering_Realms/) and the [official game website](https://witheringrealms.com/).
