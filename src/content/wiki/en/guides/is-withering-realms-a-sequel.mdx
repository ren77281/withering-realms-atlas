## Is Withering Realms a direct sequel to Withering Rooms?

No. The official Withering Realms FAQ describes the game as a **new, independent story set in the same universe** as Withering Rooms. That makes it a shared-universe follow-up from the same creator, not a continuation that requires players to know the first game's plot, characters, or ending.

## Do I need to play Withering Rooms first?

No. Moonless Formless explicitly says that familiarity with Withering Rooms is neither necessary nor expected to fully enjoy Withering Realms. New players can begin with Clover, her doll guardian, and the mystery of Penwyll without researching the previous game first.

For the confirmed premise of the new story, see the [Clover, doll, and setting guide](/characters/story-setting-clover-doll).

## What does the shared universe confirm?

The phrase confirms a common fictional universe, but the developer has not publicly identified a returning character, location, event, or direct plot thread. Withering Realms has its own protagonist, setting, and central goal: ghost child Clover explores Penwyll while seeking a way to return to life.

The official material does not establish that every supernatural rule, enemy type, item, or gameplay system from Withering Rooms carries over. Those connections should remain unconfirmed unless Moonless Formless names them directly or the released game demonstrates them.

## Are both games made by the same developer?

Yes. Moonless Formless identifies itself as a solo developer based in Seattle. Its official studio page calls Withering Rooms its first game and Withering Realms its second. The Withering Realms website and Steam page also describe the new title as being from the creator of Withering Rooms.

## Could more connections be revealed later?

Possibly, but no specific crossover has been announced. Story links, returning characters, and references may become clear through future official material or the released game. Until then, the safe conclusion is limited: same universe and creator, independent story, no required prior knowledge.
