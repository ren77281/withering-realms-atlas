## What kind of game is Withering Realms?

Withering Realms is a **horror action RPG with mystery and puzzle elements**. Its official FAQ describes a primarily three-quarter, top-down viewpoint. The game follows ghost child Clover and her doll guardian through the town of Penwyll and the dangerous areas surrounding it.

## What does the main gameplay loop include?

Official descriptions connect exploration, combat, loot, and world expansion rather than presenting them as separate modes.

1. Explore Penwyll, graveyards, tombs, caves, and rotting estates.
2. Search for treasure, upgrade materials, and magical artifacts.
3. Craft weapons or machines for the doll guardian's missing right arm.
4. Defeat massive perimeter guardians to expand the world map.
5. Revisit areas at night or after gaining traversal abilities to find additional threats and secrets.

Read the [confirmed locations guide](/maps/penwyll-confirmed-locations) for the places named by the developer, and the [doll arm equipment page](/items/doll-arm-weapons) for the examples listed by official sources.

## Is Withering Realms a roguelike?

No. The official FAQ says some areas and dungeons are randomized for variety when entered, but items, equipment, and story progress are **not lost on death**. Randomized layouts alone do not make the game a roguelike, and the developer explicitly rejects that label.

## How is the world structured?

Areas and dungeons appear on a world map that expands as the game progresses. The official material ties expansion to defeating perimeter guardians. Penwyll acts as the central town and contains shops and NPCs, while surrounding locations support combat, exploration, and revisits.

## What is not confirmed yet?

Official pages do not currently provide exact combat controls, difficulty modes, skill trees, weapon stats, drop rates, boss numbers, or a complete progression route. Those details require the released build or a new official guide. This page intentionally avoids filling those gaps with trailer guesses.
