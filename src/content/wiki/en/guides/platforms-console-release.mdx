## Is a Withering Realms PS5 version confirmed?

No. The developer has confirmed that Withering Realms is intended to come to consoles at full release, after the Steam Early Access period, but **PS5 has not been named**. Treat pages or posts that call a PS5 version confirmed as speculation unless Moonless Formless announces it directly.

## Which platform is confirmed for launch?

The currently confirmed launch is Windows PC through Steam Early Access on September 2, 2026. Steam lists Windows support and does not list macOS or Linux support. The official website summarizes the plan as “Steam (Early Access)” followed by “PC & Consoles (Full Release).”

| Platform | Current official status |
| --- | --- |
| Windows PC | Confirmed for Steam Early Access |
| PlayStation 5 | Not individually announced |
| Xbox Series X/S | Not individually announced |
| Nintendo platform | Not individually announced |
| macOS or Linux | Not listed on Steam |

## When will console platforms be announced?

Moonless Formless says the specific consoles will be announced closer to the full release date. The full-release date and the duration of Early Access are also unknown, so there is no reliable console window yet. The [release-date guide](/guides/release-date-early-access) tracks the confirmed schedule.

## Why do some pages already mention PS5?

Players reasonably connect Withering Realms with PlayStation because the developer's previous game, Withering Rooms, reached consoles. That history does not confirm the exact platform list for this game. The official Withering Realms FAQ deliberately uses the broader word “consoles.”

## How will this page be updated?

We will change the table only after a named-platform announcement appears on the official website, Steam page, or a Moonless Formless account linked by the developer. Until then, the accurate answer is: consoles are planned, PS5 is unconfirmed.
