## What loot is confirmed in Withering Realms?

The official game description names **treasure, upgrade materials, and magical artifacts** as rewards found while descending into tombs, caves, and rotting estates. These are confirmed loot categories, but the developer has not published a complete database or explained the precise boundary between them.

| Loot category | Confirmed use or context | Still unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treasure | Found while exploring dangerous locations | Contents, value, vendors, rarity |
| Upgrade materials | Used somewhere in progression | Exact upgrade targets and costs |
| Magical artifacts | Discoverable magical items | Names, effects, slots, limitations |
| Crafted arm equipment | Slots into the doll's missing right arm | Recipes, materials, stats, unlocks |

## Are upgrade materials used for doll weapons?

That connection is plausible, but it is not explicitly described in the available official text. The sources place upgrade materials and arm crafting in the same feature overview without stating that every material feeds that crafting system. A reliable recipe guide must wait for official documentation or repeatable in-game evidence.

The [doll arm weapons page](/items/doll-arm-weapons) lists only attachments named by first-party sources and flags a wording difference between the official website and Steam.

## What do magical artifacts do?

No artifact names or effects are currently published on the official website or Steam description. It would be misleading to assign artifacts to passive bonuses, spells, equipment slots, or quest functions before those mechanics are documented.

## Where should players expect to search?

Official material associates loot searches with tombs, caves, and rotting estates. It does not provide fixed chest coordinates or drop tables, and the FAQ says some areas and dungeons are randomized when entered. The [Penwyll locations guide](/maps/penwyll-confirmed-locations) explains the broader world structure.
