Pre-release searches for **Withering Realms artifacts** or upgrade materials currently lead to broad official categories, not a usable item database. Moonless Formless confirms that players descend into tombs, caves, and rotting estates in search of treasure, upgrade materials, and magical artifacts. The developer also confirms crafting for the doll guardian's missing right arm. What is missing matters just as much: there are no published artifact names, effects, slots, recipes, rarity tiers, material costs, fixed chest coordinates, or drop rates. This guide explains the confirmed loot structure, avoids merging separate systems without evidence, and defines the fields a trustworthy Early Access item guide should record.

## What loot is confirmed in Withering Realms?

The official description names **treasure, upgrade materials, and magical artifacts** as discoveries in dangerous exploration areas. These are confirmed categories, but their precise boundaries and uses are not documented.

| Loot category | Confirmed use or context | Still unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treasure | Found while exploring dangerous locations | Contents, value, vendors, rarity |
| Upgrade materials | Used somewhere in progression | Exact upgrade targets and costs |
| Magical artifacts | Discoverable magical items | Names, effects, slots, limitations |
| Crafted arm equipment | Slots into the doll's missing right arm | Recipes, materials, stats, unlocks |

## Are upgrade materials used for doll weapons?

That connection is plausible, but not explicit. The feature overview places upgrade materials and arm crafting near each other without stating that every material feeds that system—or that arm attachments are the only upgrade target.

The [confirmed doll arm weapons list](/items/doll-arm-weapons) records only attachments named by first-party sources and flags the wording difference between the website and Steam.

## What do magical artifacts do?

No artifact names or effects are currently published in the official text. It would be misleading to assign them passive bonuses, spells, equipment slots, or quest functions. Even the word “magical” describes the category without revealing how an artifact is equipped or activated.

## Where are treasure and materials found?

Official material associates loot searches with tombs, caves, and rotting estates. It does not provide fixed chest coordinates or enemy drop tables. The FAQ also says some areas and dungeons are randomized when entered, so a route may need to distinguish a stable entrance from variable interior rooms.

The [Penwyll and confirmed locations guide](/maps/penwyll-confirmed-locations) explains that broader world structure.

## What should an item database record after release?

A useful database needs enough evidence to reproduce a result:

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Item name and category | Prevents treasure, materials, and artifacts from being mixed |
| Effect or crafting use | Explains practical value without relying on rarity color |
| Source and conditions | Separates fixed rewards, random finds, vendors, and quests |
| Quantity or cost | Makes recipes and upgrades actionable |
| Game version | Preserves accuracy through Early Access balance changes |
| Verification method | Distinguishes official values from observed samples |

## Withering Realms artifacts FAQ

### Are magical artifacts confirmed?

Yes. Official descriptions name magical artifacts as exploration discoveries, but do not list individual artifacts.

### Are artifact effects known?

No. Passive bonuses, spells, slots, activation rules, and restrictions are unannounced.

### Are upgrade material locations available?

Only broad environment types are named. Exact chests, enemy drops, vendors, and repeatable farming routes are not published.

### Are crafting recipes available?

No first-party recipe list or material cost table is currently available.

### Can drop rates be calculated before release?

No reliable rates can be claimed without official values or a documented sample from the playable build, especially where randomization may affect encounters.

## What will be added after release?

Once the game is available, this page can become a source-indexed hub for item names, uses, locations, costs, and version history. Drop rates should appear only when an official value exists or the sample size and method are documented clearly.
