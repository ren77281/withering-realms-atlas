Players searching for a **Withering Realms doll arm weapons** list can find five first-party names, but they cannot yet build a reliable tier list. The official website and Steam agree that the doll guardian's missing right arm accepts crafted weapons and machines. They share Chain Rock, Machinegun, and Flamethrower, while each page contains one additional name. This guide compares both lists and separates useful pre-release facts from damage values, recipes, ammunition rules, and unlock conditions that still require the Early Access build.

## How does the doll's missing right arm work?

Official descriptions say players can **craft weapons and machines** and slot them into the doll guardian's missing right arm. That establishes an equipment system built around arm attachments. It does not establish whether players switch attachments during combat, carry several at once, consume ammunition, or improve them through a separate upgrade tree.

The wording also does not prove that every attachment is a conventional weapon. “Weapons and machines” leaves room for different functions, while the names themselves should not be converted into invented attack types or statistics.

## Is this the complete doll arm weapon list?

No. Both descriptions introduce these names as examples rather than a final catalog. Early Access is also explicitly intended for balance changes and system feedback, so the available equipment, behavior, and tuning may change before full release.

## How are doll arm attachments crafted or unlocked?

The public material confirms crafting but does not publish recipes, vendors, drop tables, unlock quests, or material costs. Official descriptions separately mention treasure, upgrade materials, and magical artifacts, but they do not say which material creates or improves a particular arm.

The [Withering Realms artifacts and upgrade materials guide](/items/artifacts-upgrade-materials) records those loot categories without assigning unsupported recipes to them.

## Can you choose the best arm before Early Access?

Not responsibly. A useful comparison needs more than an attachment name. It needs the game version, damage or utility behavior, range, resource cost, upgrade level, target type, and a reproducible test. None of those fields is available for all five named attachments.

## Withering Realms doll arm weapons FAQ

### Is the Enchanted Candelabrum confirmed?

Yes. Steam names it as an example arm attachment. Its appearance, effect, recipe, and combat role are not yet described in text.

### Is the Pivoting Greatsword still in the game?

The official website currently names it. Steam omits it, but that omission is not evidence that it was removed.

### Are Chain Rock and Machinegun separate attachments?

They are presented as separate names in both official lists. Their exact mechanics and resource rules remain unannounced.

### Can the doll use more than one arm at once?

No first-party source currently states the number of equipped or carried attachments, or whether switching is allowed during combat.

### Are exact weapon stats available?

No official damage, range, speed, ammunition, or upgrade values are published. Any numerical list before reliable game access would be speculative.

## What should a reliable arm guide verify next?

A reliable update should record where each attachment is obtained, its recipe and material cost, how switching works, what resources it consumes, its verified effects, and the tested game version. Until those details are available, the strongest answer is the five-name source comparison above.
