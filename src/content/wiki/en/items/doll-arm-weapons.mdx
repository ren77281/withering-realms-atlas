## How does the doll's missing right arm work?

Official descriptions say players can **craft weapons and machines** and slot them into the doll guardian's missing right arm. This confirms an equipment system centered on interchangeable arm attachments, but it does not yet confirm slot limits, switching rules, upgrade trees, ammunition, or exact combat values.

Because both pages are official, the safest interpretation is that they describe overlapping examples from different copy revisions. The difference does **not** prove that Pivoting Greatsword was renamed, removed, or replaced by Enchanted Candelabrum.

## Are these all the weapons in Withering Realms?

No complete equipment list has been published. The official wording introduces the named attachments as examples, not a final catalog. Additional arms may appear in Early Access, and the developer has already stated that systems and balance may change during that period.

## Where do the weapons come from?

The public material confirms crafting but does not publish recipes, vendors, drop tables, unlock quests, or material costs. The [artifacts and materials page](/items/artifacts-upgrade-materials) records the confirmed loot categories without assigning unsupported recipes to them.

## How should early build guides handle this system?

Any future “best arm” or damage comparison needs a visible game version and reproducible test method. Before release, exact DPS, range, status effects, and upgrade costs remain unknown.
