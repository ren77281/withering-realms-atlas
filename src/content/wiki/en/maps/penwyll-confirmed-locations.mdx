## Which Withering Realms locations are officially confirmed?

Moonless Formless has named Penwyll and several surrounding environment types, but has not released a complete labeled map. The currently confirmed places are enough to describe the world at a high level, not enough to publish routes or collectible coordinates.

| Place | What the official sources say |
| --- | --- |
| Penwyll | Central mysterious town with shops and NPCs |
| Decaying mansion | Where nine-year-old Clover awakens near the occult epicenter |
| Graveyards | Sprawl around Penwyll and form part of the explorable region |
| Tombs | Dungeon-like locations searched for loot |
| Caves | Explorable locations containing danger and discoveries |
| Rotting estates | Additional exploration spaces named in the feature list |

## How does the world map expand?

The official description says players expand the world map by defeating **massive perimeter guardians**. It does not reveal their names, combat phases, exact locations, or how many map boundaries exist. Those details need released-game verification.

For the larger loop connecting combat and exploration, see the [gameplay overview](/guides/gameplay-overview).

## Is Penwyll only a quest hub?

The official FAQ identifies Penwyll as the town at the center and confirms shops and NPCs. Calling it only a menu-like hub would go beyond the source: the town is also central to the story, especially Althea's investigation of its dark secret.

## Are the locations fixed or randomized?

Some areas and dungeons are randomized for variety when entered, according to the developer. The source does not identify which locations use randomization or whether their overall map positions change. Fixed walkthrough maps should therefore wait for the Early Access build.

## What can change when an area is revisited?

The developer confirms two reasons to return: nighttime can introduce new creatures and secrets, and new traversal abilities can reveal additional treasure or secrets. The [nighttime changes page](/maps/nighttime-world-changes) keeps those mechanics separate from unsupported day-night assumptions.
